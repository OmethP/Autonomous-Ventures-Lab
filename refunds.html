Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Before work begins

If you need to cancel before agreed work has started, contact us as soon as possible. Where legally and operationally possible, we generally provide a refund of amounts paid for work that has not begun, subject to any non-recoverable third-party costs that were clearly approved in advance.

After work begins

If work has already started, we will review the completed portion, agreed milestones, and any third-party costs. Where appropriate, a partial refund may be offered for undelivered work.

Completed custom work

Custom digital work that has been completed and delivered is generally not refundable solely because a customer changes their mind. If the delivered work materially differs from the agreed scope, contact us and we will first try to correct the issue or agree another fair resolution.

Disputes

Please contact us before opening a payment dispute so we can review the issue quickly. This request does not limit any legal rights you may have.

Cancellations of ongoing work

If an ongoing or recurring service is agreed, cancellation terms will be stated in the project agreement. Unless otherwise agreed, cancellation applies prospectively and does not erase payment obligations for work already completed.

Mandatory rights

Nothing in this policy limits statutory consumer rights or other mandatory rights that apply under relevant law.

Contact

Email refund or cancellation requests to autonomousventures.hq@gmail.com with the project name and payment reference if available.
