Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Autonomous Ventures Lab respects customer privacy. This policy explains the basic information we may receive when you contact us or purchase a service.

Information we may collect

We may receive your name, business name, email address, project information, messages, and transaction status. Payment card details are not requested by email and, where Stripe is used, payment details are handled by Stripe through its secure payment pages.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, deliver agreed services, provide customer support, prevent abuse, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

We may use service providers for email, AI-assisted work, cloud tools, file storage, analytics, and payment processing. We aim to share only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant service.

Retention

We keep business and project records only as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, support, accounting, legal obligations, dispute handling, and security.

Your requests

You can contact us to ask questions about personal information connected with your project and to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, subject to legal retention requirements.
